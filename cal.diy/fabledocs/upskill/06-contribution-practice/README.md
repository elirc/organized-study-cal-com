# 06 · Contribution Practice

Real work, scoped for growth. Every ticket answers: why is it useful, why is the blast radius small, what pattern does it follow, how is it tested, and what would make a maintainer reject it. No drive-by refactors.

1. [01-good-first-tickets.md](01-good-first-tickets.md) — 18 junior tickets across the repo.
2. [02-mid-level-feature-tickets.md](02-mid-level-feature-tickets.md) — 12 cross-layer tickets; design notes required.
3. [03-senior-build-projects.md](03-senior-build-projects.md) — 6 projects (2 days–4 weeks).
4. [04-refactor-and-design-katas.md](04-refactor-and-design-katas.md) — senior katas with self-grading.

Each ticket/project has an **Interview story potential** line — completing it produces a STAR story ([08-interview-prep/06](../08-interview-prep/06-behavioral-star-stories.md)). Ship 2-3 junior tickets before attempting a mid-level one; the goal is a portfolio of real contributions you can talk about.
