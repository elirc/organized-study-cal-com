# 07 · Career & Collaboration

The non-code skills that determine whether your good work lands: reviewing, writing PRs/RFCs, and communicating with maintainers. These are also interview signals — "tell me about a code review" and "how do you handle disagreement" are standard.

1. [01-code-review-mindset.md](01-code-review-mindset.md) — the review layers + a repo-specific checklist.
2. [02-writing-prs-and-rfcs.md](02-writing-prs-and-rfcs.md) — PR/commit/RFC templates tailored to this repo.
3. [03-maintainer-communication.md](03-maintainer-communication.md) — asking, proposing, disagreeing well.

Grounded in this repo's actual norms (`AGENTS.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`): small PRs, conventional commits, draft-by-default, type-check before push.
